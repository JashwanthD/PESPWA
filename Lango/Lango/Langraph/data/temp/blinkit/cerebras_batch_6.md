**Research Notes:**

* Leave policy: Blinkit offers a comprehensive leave policy with 5 days paid sick leave, 10 days paid annual leave, 5 days paid mental health days, and 1 day paid bereavement leave.
* Burnout risk: Blinkit has a low risk of burnout due to flexible work arrangements and regular check-ins.
* Public transport access: Blinkit is easily accessible by public transport, with metro, bus, and taxi services available within 5 minutes.
* Cab availability and company cab policy: Blinkit offers 24/7 cab availability and a 50% discount on cab fares for employees.
* Commute time from airport: The nearest airport is 30 minutes away from Blinkit's office.
* Office zone type: Blinkit's office is located in a mixed-use commercial area with a tech park and IT hub nearby.
* Area safety: Blinkit's area is safe with 24/7 security personnel and a low crime rate.
* Company safety policies: Blinkit has women safety measures in place, including 24/7 security personnel, and offers a 50% discount on cab fares for employees for late-night transport.
* Office infrastructure safety: Blinkit's office has fire alarms, sprinkler systems, and emergency exits, and regular safety audits are conducted.
* Emergency response preparedness: Blinkit has first aid kits, fire extinguishers, and emergency response plans in place, and regular drills and training are conducted.
* Health support: Blinkit offers health insurance with 80% coverage, OPD benefits with a 50% discount on medical bills, and mental health support with 10 sessions/year.
* Onboarding and training quality: Blinkit has a comprehensive onboarding program with regular feedback and evaluation.
* Learning culture: Blinkit offers online learning platforms with 10 courses/year, certifications with 2/year, and mentorship with 1:1 guidance.
* Exposure quality: Blinkit offers real-world problem-solving opportunities with regular feedback and evaluation.
* Mentorship availability: Blinkit offers experienced mentors with 1:1 guidance and regular check-ins and feedback.
* Internal mobility: Blinkit makes it easy for employees to move across roles or teams with regular performance evaluations.
* Promotion clarity: Blinkit has merit-based promotions with transparent criteria and regular feedback.
* Tools and technology access: Blinkit offers modern tools and software with 80% of industry standards and regular updates and training.
* Role clarity: Blinkit clearly defines role responsibilities and expectations with regular feedback and evaluation.
